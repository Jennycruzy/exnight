Sell before the dividend? Usually, don't.

When a stock pays a dividend, its Bitget rToken drops overnight by about the dividend amount. Selling first and buying back looks like free money. Exnight checks whether it really is, for each event and trade size, after tax and trading costs. Every decision is locked in before the market moves and graded afterwards.

How it works

Three facts decide the answer.

Exnight measures each one from Bitget data, then compares them. If selling first doesn't clearly win, it tells you to hold.

01 · THE DROP

The price falls by the dividend

Across 127 past events, by 04:00 ET on the ex-date an rToken had fallen by about the full dividend.

0.97× the dividend
02 · THE TAX

But you only keep part of it

US dividends to rToken holders can have up to 30% withheld. Selling first avoids the drop, and gives up only what you'd have kept.

70% kept after 30% tax
03 · THE COST

Selling and buying back costs money

Two trades mean two fees plus slippage. The gap between the drop and what you keep has to beat this.

~45 bps round trip

Proof

What the evidence shows

  • The overnight dividend drop is real and repeatable: about 0.97× the dividend.
  • Exnight is +32.5 bps per event ahead of selling first, on data the rule had never seen.
  • After tax and costs, selling first loses. Always doing it lost at every cost from 10 to 100 bps and every tax rate from 0% to 30%.
  • A real $10 order filled in full at the quote on an rToken with an empty public order book, at a 10 bps fee.
  • Every decision is committed to Git before the cutoff, so it can't be changed after the result is known.

Scope and limits

  • EXIT is rare by design: it needs roughly 70 bp of yield and a tight drop estimate. No past event qualified; the live high-yield events are the first real test.
  • Past trading costs are modeled. Historical order books don't exist.
  • Trades of $1,000 and up use modelled costs; one $10 order has filled for real.
  • The live test is still running, and a handful of events can't prove much alone.

Plain-English glossary

Four words you'll see in the app.

EX-DATE
The day the price drops by the dividend. You must hold the token before it to get paid.
HOLD
Keep the token through the ex-date. Selling first would cost more than it saves.
EXIT
Sell before the cutoff and buy back after. Only when that clearly wins after tax and costs.
NO_SIGNAL
Not enough evidence to decide, for example a trade too big for the visible quotes. Exnight won't guess.
