Common Question About Bitget Stock Spot Trading: rTokens, Dividends, Fees and Trading Hours

2026-06-23 07:3915230

[Estimated reading time: 10 mins]

This article explains how rTokens work, how dividends are handled, what fees may apply, supported trading hours, asset backing, custody, eligibility, and how Bitget stock spot trading fits into the Bitget ecosystem.

 

1. What is Bitget stock spot trading?

Bitget stock spot trading is Bitget’s upgraded tokenized-stock spot product. It allows eligible users to trade Reality-issued rTokens linked to selected U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs with USDT.

The product supports market-linked liquidity, separate USDT dividend distributions, corporate-action handling, and broader use across selected Bitget products.

2. What is Reality Protocol?

Reality Protocol is the real-world asset infrastructure behind Bitget stock spot trading. It connects rTokens to underlying securities through licensed brokerage, custody, reserve verification, settlement, and smart-contract infrastructure.

Reality handles the issuance and backing layer, while Bitget provides the trading environment, order books, USDT markets, account access, and supported product integrations.

3. What are rTokens?

rTokens are blockchain-based assets that provide economic exposure to traditional securities. Each rToken uses an “r” prefix before the underlying ticker, such as rAAPL, rTSLA, rNVDA, or rQQQ.

Each rToken is designed to track the economic value of one corresponding stock or ETF share. However, holding an rToken is not the same as directly owning a registered share and generally does not include shareholder voting rights.

4. Which U.S. stocks and ETFs are supported?

Bitget stock spot trading supports selected stock- and ETF-linked assets. Examples may include rTokens linked to companies such as Apple, Tesla, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta.

ETF-linked examples may include QQQ and SPY. The supported asset list may change, so users should check the current Bitget Stocks market page before trading.

5. How is Bitget stock spot trading different from earlier stock-token products?

Bitget stock spot trading uses Reality-issued rTokens as the infrastructure for the upgraded stock spot experience. This is different from earlier third-party tokenized-stock products.

The main improvements include deeper market-linked liquidity, cleaner dividend and corporate-action processing, and broader use of eligible stock-linked assets across Bitget’s trading and capital-management ecosystem.

6. Can I buy U.S. stock exposure with USDT?

Yes. Eligible users can trade supported Reality-issued rTokens with USDT through Bitget stock spot trading.

For users who already hold stablecoins, this provides a more direct way to access U.S. stock-linked exposure without funding a separate USD brokerage account.

7. Do I need to convert HKD or RMB into USD first?

Not if you already hold USDT. Eligible users can trade supported rTokens without completing an additional broker-side HKD- or RMB-to-USD conversion.

However, users who need to acquire USDT first may still face conversion, payment-provider, or funding costs.

8. Does Bitget stock spot trading eliminate foreign-exchange fees?

Bitget stock spot trading can reduce an extra USD conversion step for users who already hold USDT, but it does not remove every possible cost.

Users may still encounter USDT acquisition costs, bid-ask spreads, trading fees, withdrawal fees, blockchain network fees, or charges from banks and payment providers.

9. Do I need a foreign bank account or foreign bank card?

Eligible users do not need to maintain a separate foreign brokerage balance to trade supported rTokens on Bitget.

Existing crypto holders can fund their account through an on-chain USDT transfer. Users purchasing USDT with fiat may still need a supported bank account, card, or payment provider.

10. How do I start trading rTokens on Bitget?

Eligible users can start by completing the following steps:

  1. Create a Bitget account.

  2. Complete the required identity verification.

  3. Deposit or acquire USDT through a supported method.

  4. Open the U.S. stocks trading section.

  5. Select a supported rToken.

  6. Place a market or limit order.

Users can then monitor their positions, trading activity, and eligible dividend distributions in their Bitget account.

11. What fees apply to Bitget stock spot trading?

Bitget stock spot trading applies maker and taker fees to rToken trades. Users may also encounter other costs depending on how they fund, trade, or withdraw assets.

These costs may include bid-ask spreads, USDT acquisition costs, withdrawal fees, blockchain network fees, and asset- or product-specific charges. Users should check the latest Bitget fee page before trading.

12. Is trading rTokens cheaper than using a traditional broker?

It can be more cost-efficient for users who already hold USDT, especially if it removes an additional foreign-exchange or overseas-account funding step.

However, total cost depends on the user’s funding method, order size, market liquidity, trading fees, and spreads. Bitget stock spot trading may reduce friction, but it is not automatically cheaper in every case.

13. Are there hidden currency-conversion fees?

Supported rTokens trade against USDT, so users do not need to maintain a separate USD balance inside Bitget stock spot trading.

However, acquiring USDT may involve spreads or service fees charged by a bank, exchange, card provider, or payment partner. Users should compare the final USDT purchase price and all applicable trading and withdrawal costs.

14. Does Bitget charge a dividend collection fee?

Eligible cash dividends are processed automatically and credited in USDT. Users should review the current product terms for any applicable processing charges, taxes, withholding, or other deductions.

The main benefit is convenience, as Bitget stock spot trading handles the dividend workflow at the product level.

15. Does Bitget stock spot trading pay dividends?

Yes. Eligible net cash dividends from supported underlying securities are converted into USDT and credited separately to the user’s Bitget account.

The distribution amount and timing are shown independently from the rToken’s market price, making it easier to distinguish dividend income from price performance.

16. How does Reality Protocol handle stock dividends?

Reality Protocol uses a clean-price model. Eligible cash dividends are distributed separately in stablecoins instead of being automatically reinvested into the rToken.

This helps keep one rToken easy to compare with one corresponding share. Supported stock dividends and corporate actions are reflected through adjustments to the user’s token position.

17. Does Reality eliminate W-8BEN paperwork?

Reality Protocol can reduce much of the user-facing paperwork associated with traditional brokerage dividends because eligible net distributions are processed at the product level and credited in USDT.

However, documentation requirements may still depend on the issuer, platform, account structure, tax residence, and applicable law.

18. Does Reality eliminate the 30% U.S. dividend tax?

Reality Protocol does not universally eliminate U.S. dividend withholding. If withholding applies, it may be deducted before the eligible net dividend is credited in USDT.

The applicable rate depends on the product structure, relevant parties, tax treaties, and the user’s circumstances.

19. Are dividends received in USDT tax-free?

No. Receiving a dividend in USDT does not automatically make it tax-free.

The distribution may already be net of withholding, and users may still have reporting or payment obligations in their country of tax residence. Tax treatment depends on local law, issuer structure, applicable treaties, and personal circumstances.

20. Can I reinvest my USDT dividends?

Yes. Eligible cash dividends are credited separately in USDT, giving users control over how to use them.

Users may choose to reinvest in the same rToken, buy another supported stock-linked asset, hold USDT, use the funds in another eligible Bitget product, or withdraw the USDT.

21. Are Reality rTokens backed by real U.S. stocks?

Reality states that each rToken is backed by the corresponding underlying security held through licensed brokerage and segregated reserve custody infrastructure.

This structure is designed to ensure that circulating rTokens are supported by underlying holdings rather than relying only on synthetic price exposure.

22. Do rToken holders own the underlying shares?

No. rToken holders receive contractual economic exposure to the referenced security, but they do not generally become registered shareholders of the underlying company.

This means users typically do not receive shareholder voting rights, entry on the company’s shareholder register, direct shareholder information rights, or delivery of the underlying shares.

23. How are Reality’s reserves verified?

Reality uses a Proof of Asset framework and independent reserve attestations to compare the number of rTokens in circulation with the securities held in reserve.

These controls are designed to improve transparency across both the on-chain token supply and the off-chain reserve structure.

24. Can rTokens be redeemed?

Reality’s infrastructure supports the minting and redemption of eligible rTokens using stablecoins.

Users may also sell supported rTokens through Bitget’s secondary market. Redemption availability, timing, fees, minimum amounts, and settlement conditions may vary by asset, venue, account type, and jurisdiction.

25. What happens if a company completes a stock split or merger?

Supported stock splits, reverse splits, mergers, and other corporate actions are reflected through adjustments to the relevant rToken position.

Reality is designed to keep the user’s economic exposure aligned with the updated share structure. Processing times may depend on the specific corporate event and trading venue.

26. Is Bitget stock spot trading available 24/7?

Bitget stock spot trading currently supports extended trading across a 24/5 schedule rather than guaranteed 24/7 access.

Reality Protocol covers pre-market, regular market, post-market, and overnight sessions, giving eligible users a wider trading window than standard U.S. market hours alone. Weekend access and U.S. market holidays may be limited.

27. Can I trade rTokens outside regular U.S. market hours?

Yes. Supported rTokens may be available during pre-market, post-market, and overnight sessions, subject to current product support and market conditions.

Liquidity may be lower during extended hours, and temporary pauses can occur during session changes, corporate actions, or periods of unusual volatility.

28. Where does rToken liquidity come from?

Reality Protocol connects underlying transactions to U.S. equity-market liquidity through licensed brokerage infrastructure. Bitget adds its own order books, trading systems, active user base, and execution environment.

Reality also supports Direct Market Access and Request for Quote models, which are designed to improve order-book depth and reduce slippage.

29. Can an rToken trade above or below the underlying stock price?

Yes. Reality is designed to keep each rToken closely aligned with its corresponding underlying security, but temporary price differences can still occur.

Possible causes include bid-ask spreads, extended-hours liquidity, market volatility, session transitions, venue-specific order flow, corporate actions, or temporary operational pauses.

30. Can rTokens be used as margin?

Eligible rTokens may be integrated with Bitget’s unified or multi-asset margin systems, depending on the asset, account type, jurisdiction, and current product availability.

Margin use introduces additional risks, including liquidation, leverage, and collateral haircuts. Users should review the applicable risk parameters before using an rToken as collateral.

31. Can rTokens be used in grid trading or copy trading?

Eligible rTokens may be supported in Bitget’s spot grid, futures grid, and copy-trading tools, depending on current product availability.

Strategy tools do not guarantee returns, and automated trading can increase losses during volatile or illiquid market conditions. Users should confirm that the selected rToken is supported before creating a strategy.

32. Can rTokens earn yield?

Selected rTokens may be eligible for lending, Earn, wealth-management, or other yield products within Bitget.

Availability, rates, lock-up periods, redemption terms, and regional access may vary. Yield is not guaranteed, and users should review the terms of the specific product before participating.

33. Can I deposit or withdraw rTokens?

Supported rTokens are designed as transferable on-chain assets, and Bitget may enable deposits and withdrawals for eligible tokens and networks.

Support can vary by token, blockchain, jurisdiction, account type, and platform status. Users should always confirm the correct network and contract address before transferring funds.

34. What are the risks of using rTokens as collateral?

Using rTokens as collateral introduces risks beyond simply holding an unleveraged stock-linked position.

These risks may include liquidation risk, leverage risk, collateral haircuts, oracle delays or errors, liquidity risk, smart-contract risk, custody and platform risk, and sharp price moves outside regular market hours.

35. Who can trade on Bitget stock spot trading?

Bitget stock spot trading is available to eligible Bitget users who complete the required identity verification and meet the relevant regional, account, and product requirements.

Availability may vary by country, residence, physical location, asset, and account entity. Users should check the latest Bitget product page and regional terms before trading.

36. Is Bitget stock spot trading available to U.S. persons?

No. Reality-issued rTokens are not generally offered in the United States or to U.S. persons.

Access remains subject to Bitget’s compliance requirements, applicable securities laws, and regional restrictions. Users must not acquire rTokens on behalf of an ineligible person.

37. Can mainland Chinese residents use Bitget stock spot trading?

Eligible mainland Chinese residents may be able to access Bitget stock spot trading if the product is available for their account and they satisfy Bitget’s identity-verification, regional, and product requirements.

Availability can depend on the user’s location, residence, account entity, and applicable law. Users are responsible for confirming that their access and trading activity comply with local requirements.

38. Who is Bitget stock spot trading suitable for?

Bitget stock spot trading may be suitable for eligible users who already hold USDT and want U.S. stock-linked exposure inside Bitget.

It may also suit users who prefer eligible net dividends credited in USDT, value extended 24/5 trading access, want crypto and stock-linked assets in one account, or are interested in supported margin, grid, copy-trading, lending, or yield tools.

39. When might a traditional broker be better?

A traditional broker may be more suitable for users who prioritize direct or conventional beneficial share ownership, shareholder voting rights, options trading, full-service securities products, or a familiar local investor-protection framework.

Bitget stock spot trading is designed for crypto-native users who value stablecoin settlement, extended access, and broader capital utility. It complements traditional brokerage access rather than replacing every feature of a conventional securities account.

40. What should users check before trading on Bitget Spot Stock Trading?

Before trading, users should check whether the product is available in their region and whether their account meets the relevant requirements.

Users should also review supported assets, trading hours, fees, dividend rules, tax considerations, collateral settings, deposit and withdrawal support, and the risks of trading tokenized stock-linked assets.
