Announcement: Dividend Distribution Completed for 63 Stocks Including rMU, rQQQ, and rTSM

2026-07-24 01:4212598
We are pleased to announce that the dividend distribution for 63 stocks with payment dates on or before July 22 (EST) has been fully completed. Bitget has successfully settled and credited the USDT dividends proportionally to users who held the relevant assets at the time of the snapshot. The entire process was fully automated, requiring no further action from users.
This distribution covers a diverse range of US stocks and ETF assets, including popular representative tickers such as rMU, rQQQ, and rTSM.

Distribution Rules

  • Withholding Tax: In accordance with US tax regulations, the securities custodian will deduct a 30% federal withholding tax on dividend income.
  • Actual Received Amount = Number of Shares Held × Dividend per Share × 70%
  • Eligibility: Users holding the corresponding assets at the time of the snapshot will receive the dividends automatically. No manual operation is required.

Important Notes on Crediting

Dividend distribution is contingent upon the platform's actual receipt of funds from the securities custodian. Once received, dividends are automatically credited to the user's Spot account (Funding account). Users can check the details in their transaction history.
Please note that because funds must be processed by the custodian before being transferred, the actual crediting time may be later than the official corporate payment date. Furthermore, due to variations in tax deductions, market rules, upstream brokers, custodians, and clearing agencies, the final amount credited to the user's account is subject to the actual receipt of funds and the figures displayed on the platform.
