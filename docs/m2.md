# M2 — Issuer-verified sample and the 20:00 vs 04:00 rung (2026-09-17)

M1 used the 58 events whose gross basis came from the 2026-07-24 notice. `exnight.basis`
verifies gross basis per event against issuer data (Nasdaq declared, Yahoo realised) and
admits 109 more; this note re-runs the event study on the resolved ledger. Artifacts are
tagged `_resolved` and do not replace the M1 files:

    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.basis
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.eventstudy --ledger data/ledger/reality_notice59_resolved.jsonl --output data/results/event_results_resolved.json
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.confounders --results data/results/event_results_resolved.json --output data/results/confounders_resolved.csv
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.analysis --results data/results/event_results_resolved.json --confounders data/results/confounders_resolved.csv --tag _resolved

## 1. Sample — OBSERVED

185 actions → 127 usable (58 tier 1, 69 tier 2/3). 45 of the 69 are rSATA daily payments
(yield 0.05 %); the other 24 are 12 symbols, mostly monthly ETFs and the September quarterlies.
Corrections that come with issuer verification: rNXPI 2026-06-24 and rMDT 2026-06-26 enter with
gross 1.014 / 0.72 — the Reality feed carries the gross amount; it was the *notice* that printed
the net (0.8619 / 0.54). Those notice figures show withholding of 15 % and 25 % on those two
issuers, i.e. the rate varies by domicile (this is why net entitlement carries a range).

## 3. What this supports

- **By 04:00 ET the rToken has dropped by the gross dividend in every cut** (0.84–1.17, tight
  in the tier-2 sample). This is the durable measurement.
- **The 20:00 ET print is fully adjusted in June/early July (0.98 ± 0.10, n=51) and not
  reliably adjusted afterwards.** The pooled 0.66 is a mixture of those two, not a price level,
  and is not evidence of net-of-withholding pricing. M1's 1.11 was correct for its sample and
  does not generalise to later events.
- The post-July sample is small (19 non-rSATA events). Six of the nine August events are Monday
  ex-dates (weekend gap, already known not to adjust Friday 20:00); rCLOI/rHYS 20:00 bars are
  700 minutes stale (no trades, not "no adjustment"); September is mixed (rBITI, rSGOV, rNXPI
  adjusted at 20:00; rKO, rMO, rSTRC not until 04:00). Whether this is a venue change (the 24/7
  weekend session began 2026-07-17) or thin overnight quoting is not established.
- Consequence for the strategy: the 04:00 rung is the rung whose estimate holds across
  periods; the 20:00 rung's estimate depends on which period is used. The ex-ante verdicts
  for rAVGO/rVST 2026-09-21 are HOLD under either (fees dominate a 0.1–0.2 % yield). The
  frozen `strategy_v1` should name its rung and its estimation sample explicitly.
