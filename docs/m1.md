# M1 — Reality ledger, confounders, clean sample (2026-09-17)

M0 used the 63-row payment notice of 24 July as its event universe. M1 rebuilds the
universe from Bitget's Reality corporate-action feed for the same 59 assets and adds a
deterministic confounder review. Every number below is reproducible with:

The current counts below are from the corrected artifacts installed on 2026-09-17. The older
pre-fix results are retained only as a comparison and must not be used for a trading decision.

    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.calendar --source reality --start-date 2026-06-01 --output data/ledger/reality_notice59.jsonl --base-coin ... (59 live baseCoins)
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.eventstudy --ledger data/ledger/reality_notice59.jsonl --output data/results/event_results_reality.json
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.confounders --results data/results/event_results_reality.json --output data/results/confounders_reality.csv
    .venv/bin/python -m exnight.analysis --results data/results/event_results_reality.json --confounders data/results/confounders_reality.csv --tag _reality

## 1. What the Reality feed changed — OBSERVED

- 185 actions since 2026-06-01 for the 59 assets: 182 cash dividends, 2 reverse splits (rSOXS,
  rTZA on 2026-07-15), 1 split (rAPH 2026-09-03). 28 are pending (ex-dates up to 2027-03).
- All 63 notice rows match a Reality cash row on the same ex-date. The notice was a *partial*
  list: 22 more cash events fall inside its own window (rSATA pays daily; rSTRC monthly).
- Reality rounds `dividendPerShare` to 3–4 dp; the notice carries up to 8 dp. Differences
  within 0.5% are treated as rounding (rUPRO, rPAVE, rSTRC).
- **Two notice amounts are net of home-country withholding, not gross:** rNXPI 0.8619 vs
  1.014 (0.85) and rMDT 0.54 vs 0.72 (0.75). M0's PDR denominator for those two events was
  too small. Both stay `UNRESOLVED` and are excluded here. Yahoo flags the same pattern on
  rTSM (0.78) and rSTM (0.85); those carry the `underlying_check_failed` flag.
- Market closures now come from `/api/v3/reality/market/calendar` (no hardcoded holidays).
- Gross basis is assigned only when that exact symbol and ex-date match the saved notice
  within the documented rounding tolerance. A match on another event for the same symbol
  no longer supplies a tax basis for an unmatched event.
- The analysis reports a separate market-adjusted slope from the saved proxy return as a
  confounder check; strategy estimates continue to use the actual token move.

Exclusions are all principled: ex-date before the current listing's `openTime` (rSATA
before 2026-07-07, rSOXS/rTZA before their 2026-07-15 re-listing, rAPH before 2026-09-03),
unresolved basis (rNXPI, rMDT), non-cash actions, and pending events with no post bars.

Confounder flags (`exnight/confounders.py`, each with an evidence label): gross-basis
conflict, another cash dividend within ±1 day, non-cash action within 30 days, listing
after the event, ex-date after a weekend/holiday, stale pre bar, |rSPY move| > 1%,
yield > 2%, Yahoo disagreement, leveraged/inverse name pattern (ASSUMED heuristic).

## 4. What this supports

- At 20:00 ET the rToken has repriced by approximately the **gross** dividend
  (1.11 ± 0.12), the same as the underlying's close→open drop (1.01 ± 0.42). H₀ (rToken
  PDR = underlying PDR) is not rejected. The 04:00 and 09:30 rungs add nothing: the
  adjustment is complete in the first overnight bar.
- The one sub-sample that reads below 1.0 (confounder-clean, 0.62 ± 0.21) has r² 0.26 and
  its intersection with the tight-gap cut reads 1.22 ± 0.40. No stable sub-sample supports
  a net-of-withholding reading.
- Economic consequence for a holder: selling before 20:00 and buying back after captures
  ≈ (PDR − 0.70) × D, i.e. ≈ 0.4 D at the point estimate, ≈ 0.16 D at the 2σ lower bound.
  Median dividend yield in the sample is 0.29 % of price, so that edge is ≈ 12 bp
  (point) / ≈ 5 bp (lower bound) before a 20 bp round-trip taker fee. It clears the fee
  on 6 of 58 events at the lower bound, all with yield ≥ 1 %. The strategy verdict is
  therefore HOLD on the broad universe; EXIT is only arguable on high-yield ETF and
  monthly-payer names, which are the ones the confounder flags remove. See
  `data/results/signals_expost.csv`: with the realised drop known, the EXIT rule has a
  positive net edge on 3 of 38 priced events.
