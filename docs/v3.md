# Strategy V3 — entitlement-aware dividend wedge

V3 is a new, separately versioned rule. V1, V2, the frozen competition manifest and the
published scorecard are unchanged.

## Why a V3

V1 issues EXIT only if stepping out wins even when the holder would have kept the **whole**
gross dividend. That turns the rule into a test of whether rTokens fall by *more* than the
dividend. Exnight's thesis is narrower: the token reprices by about the gross dividend,
while the holder may retain only the net. The tradable quantity is

```text
expected drop − documented retained dividend − round-trip cost
```

V3 tests that quantity, and only where the retained dividend is documented before the
decision.

## What changes

| | V1 | V3 |
|---|---|---|
| Withholding | one global range 0–30% | per event: exactly 30% when a registered Bitget distribution notice, available before the decision, already applied the 30% rule to the same symbol at its gross amount; otherwise 0–30% |
| Expected drop | `(pdr − 2·se) × gross` | `min(pdr − 2·se, 1.0) × gross`, so no credit for overshoot |
| Estimate | one frozen coefficient | registered expanding window, fixed 04:00 ET rung, refit at each decision |
| Reporting | edges | edges plus each event's break-even gross yield |

Unchanged: `z = 2`, the gross-basis tiers, sessions, live fees, depth-walk costs,
notionals and BUY suppression.

## What V3 can and cannot show

With withholding exactly 30%, EXIT needs

```text
gross / price  >  cost fraction / (min(pdr − 2·se, 1) − 0.70)
```

so the rule can issue EXIT only when the conservative drop estimate exceeds 0.70. Even then
it applies only to high-yield events. At 0.1% taker fees on both legs, the cost fraction is
at least 20 bp before slippage. The typical historical dividend is about 5 bp of the price.
V3 will therefore be HOLD on most events by construction. Its value lies in the rare
high-yield events, such as monthly-distribution ETFs and special dividends.

## Evidence discipline

- The rule file `strategy/strategy_v3.json` was committed before any V3 estimate,
  decision or diagnostic was computed.
- The design followed V1's results and the 23 September source audit. Every historical V3
  figure is therefore a **POST_HOC_DIAGNOSTIC**, not out-of-sample evidence. Only events with
  ex-dates after registration count.
- A notice's displayed date is not treated as its publication time. The 89-stock notice
  displays 24 July but lists August payments. Primary availability is the time we can prove
  we held the page. The later of the displayed date and the last listed payment date is
  reported only as a sensitivity.

## Results so far

### Historical diagnostic — post hoc, not OOS

Produced by `.venv/bin/python -m exnight.strategy_v3 historical`. It uses the same folds, T0/T1
times, 25 bp modeled slippage, fees and concurrency cap as the competition scorecard. The
sample is 72 events: the 56 frozen pre-decision events plus the 16 issuer-verified additions
from the 23 September audit.

| Fold | Estimate at fixed 04:00 rung | Training events | Lower ratio | EXIT | HOLD | NO_SIGNAL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| August | 1.258 ± 0.474 | 33 | 0.311 | 0 | 25 | 0 |
| September | 0.944 ± 0.352 | 58 | 0.240 | 0 | 14 | 0 |

The verdicts are identical under both notice-availability modes and both retained-dividend
assumptions (`data/results/v3_historical_diagnostic.json`). Active return is zero.

Two things follow:

1. **The audit's 1.733 before-September estimate was a rung-selection effect.** With the
   rung fixed at 04:00, the same 58 events give 0.944 ± 0.352. The 1.733 figure came from the
   selection rule moving to the 09:30 open.
2. **No historical event could EXIT, even with a perfect estimate.** The largest gross yield
   among the 72 events is 52 bp. The best case is a lower ratio of 1.0 with documented 30%
   withholding, where EXIT needs a yield above `cost / 0.30`. At the modeled costs, that is
   67–150 bp.

### Forward decisions

Produced by `.venv/bin/python -m exnight.strategy_v3 forward` and written to
`data/results/signals_v3.csv` and `run_manifest_v3.json`.

The forward estimate uses all 127 resolved events: `0.966 ± 0.183`, which gives a lower ratio of
**0.599**. That is below the 0.70 share a documented holder retains, so the break-even yield is
infinite. **No event can EXIT under V3 until the estimate tightens.** Every event with
resolved inputs is HOLD. The rest are NO_SIGNAL because their gross basis is unresolved or
depth samples are missing.

### What would have to be true for V3 to issue an EXIT

- The lower ratio `pdr − 2·se` must rise above 0.70. At the current point estimate, that
  requires the standard error to fall from 0.183 to below 0.133. That needs many more
  resolved events with clean 04:00 prints, and the point estimate must hold.
- The event must also be large: about 70 bp or more of gross yield at 0.1% fees on both legs.
  In practice that means monthly-distribution income ETFs (rQQQI, rJEPQ) and special
  dividends.

This is a real limitation, not a coding result. V3 records it as the answer to the
question it was registered to ask.
