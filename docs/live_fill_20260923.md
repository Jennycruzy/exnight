One real, operator-approved order was placed to test whether a Reality token with an **empty
public order book** actually fills at its ticker quote, and at what cost. It follows Strategy
V3's recommendation for the rTOWN ex-date (25 September): **hold** through the ex-date.

## What this shows

- **Routed liquidity fills.** rTOWN had no public order book, yet a marketable limit order at
  the ticker's ask filled immediately and completely, one tick better than the limit.
- **Measured cost at this size was below the model.** The walk-forward scorecard models 25 bps
  of round-trip slippage on top of fees. This buy leg paid 10 bps in fees and no slippage.

## What it does not show

- It is one $10 order. Capacity at $1k, $5k and $25k is still unproven.
- It is one leg. The sell leg has not been exercised, so round-trip cost is not measured.
- It is not an EXIT. V3 said HOLD, and the position is held through the ex-date.

## Still to observe

rTOWN pays its dividend on **9 October 2026**. The declared gross dividend is $0.28 per share,
so this position is entitled to $0.0779 gross. The amount actually credited will show the
withholding Bitget applies to this holding: $0.0545 at 30%, $0.0779 at 0%. That is the main
uncertainty in V3's entitlement model.
